Short-epitope ranking
Each peptide takes its best eligible allele. Lower ranking cost is better. Mutation summaries aggregate distinct peptides after best-allele reduction. In epitope mode, aggregation does not change peptide ranks. Mean and median describe only the eligible subset when the binding gate is enabled.
